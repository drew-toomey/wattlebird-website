About the game

One line: Run a cosy little corner comic shop: unpack every delivery, shelve every comic by hand and find the perfect comic for anyone who asks.

Comic Corner is a cosy comic shop game. You've just taken over the old corner comic shop. Unpack the deliveries, shelve every comic by hand, find the perfect comic for anyone who asks and survive the New Comic Wednesday rush. Earn Pows to grow your shop and make it your own. No stress, ever.

  • Unpack and shelve every comic by hand
  • Dozens of made-up comic series, each with its own cover
  • Find the perfect comic for anyone who asks
  • Survive New Comic Wednesday
  • Stretch your shelves wider, taller and round the corner
  • Make it yours: name, logo, shopkeeper, floors and walls
  • No fail state. Your shop can't go bust

About the studio

Wattlebird Games is a one-person studio in Australia. Comic Corner is our first game. It's built with a strict "no AI art" rule: every model, cover and shop in the game is made by hand-written code, in our own style. (We do use AI tools to help with the code, and the in-game radio music is made with Google Flow Music, an AI music tool. We say so on Steam.)

"I've always been a reader, but comics had something books didn't. The art did half the storytelling. … So when it came time to choose my first game, the idea came easily: what if you could open your own little comic shop and fill it with that same atmosphere?"

"I lost a lot of my childhood to the Theme games by Bullfrog Productions. … I'm still a simulation and strategy fan at heart, but lately I've found myself reaching for cosier games. Comic Corner sits right in the middle of those two loves."

Drew, solo dev at Wattlebird Games (from Dev blog #01)
